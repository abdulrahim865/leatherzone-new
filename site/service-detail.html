Bespoke furniture built from scratch in our Al Quoz workshop — sofas, headboards, dining tables and more. Leather Zone Upholstery LLC has been designing and manufacturing furniture for Dubai homes, hotels, restaurants and offices for two decades, and every piece that leaves the workshop is the work of the same small team that scoped it on site.

We start with a free consultation in your space — listening first, sketching afterwards, only quoting once we are confident the piece is right for the room. We source full-grain leather and contract-grade fabrics from European mills you have heard of and show you the swatches before a single cut is made. Most pieces are designed, manufactured and installed within four to eight weeks.

Custom furniture built end-to-end in Dubai

From a single dining chair to a complete villa fit-out, the workflow is the same: on-site brief, drawings and a written quote, materials approval, manufacture at our Al Quoz workshop, finishing, then delivery and installation by our own team. No middlemen, no factory hand-offs, no surprises on hand-over day.

What we manufacture

Sofas, sectionals & lounge seating: Frame-up builds in any size, depth and configuration — Chesterfield to clean-line modular.

Headboards and bed sets: King-size upholstered headboards, fluted leather panels, integrated side tables and matching ottomans.

Dining tables & chairs: Solid-wood and veneered tables in custom dimensions, paired with upholstered or all-wood chairs.

Cabinets, consoles and bar units: Full carpentry plus stone, brass or leather inlay finishing.

Booth seating & banquettes: Restaurant and hotel-grade benches with contract-rated foam and FR fabric.

Office furniture: Boardroom tables, reception desks, executive seating and meeting-room banquettes.

Materials we work with every day

Full-grain Italian and German leathers, French and Belgian fabrics, solid walnut, oak and ash, veneered MDF panels for stable large surfaces, brass and steel hardware, marble and quartz tops, and acoustic foam for seating that survives daily use. We hold a working stock of common upholstery materials so quotes can move from approval to manufacture inside a week.
